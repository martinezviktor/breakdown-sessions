    About

    Breakdown Sessions is a podcast in English and Spanish devoted to analysis and conversation about rock and metal.

    In each episode we explore songs, albums, and bands from different angles: songwriting, production, lyrics, context, and what's happening in the scene. We also share personal opinions, our experience as musicians, and thoughts on where the genre is today.

    A place to talk about music with judgment and without hype. Just honest conversations about the bands and sounds shaping this generation.

    Hosted by Victor Martinez · New episodes every week
